✓ Recommended Improvements

Add: FAQ Section

Q: How do I convert my cigarette intake to pouch strength?
A: A useful rule of thumb: every 5 cigarettes per day roughly equals one strength tier. If you smoke 5 cigarettes daily, start with Normal (4–8 mg). At 10 per day, try Strong (8–12 mg). A pack-a-day smoker should begin with Extra Strong (12–16 mg). These are starting points — adjust based on how satisfied you feel after the first 3–5 pouches.
Q: Does the pouch format affect how strong it feels?
A: Yes. Moisture level, pouch size, and pH all influence perceived strength. A moist 6 mg pouch releases nicotine faster than a dry 6 mg pouch, making it feel stronger initially. Slim pouches distribute nicotine over a larger gum area, creating a more gradual sensation. The mg number is your best comparison metric, but expect slight variation between formats.
Q: Can I use different strengths throughout the day?
A: Absolutely — many experienced users do exactly this. A popular pattern is a stronger pouch (8–12 mg) first thing in the morning when cravings peak, then lighter pouches (4–6 mg) during the afternoon. This mimics natural nicotine patterns and can help reduce overall daily intake.
Q: What if I chose too strong — what should I do?
A: Remove the pouch immediately if you feel nauseous, dizzy, or uncomfortably buzzy. Drink water, sit down, and wait 15–20 minutes — symptoms resolve quickly. Next time, drop one full tier (e.g., from Strong to Normal). There is no benefit to pushing through discomfort.
