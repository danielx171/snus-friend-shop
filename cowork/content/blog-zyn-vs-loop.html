ZYN vs LOOP: Which Is Better in 2026?

If you're shopping for nicotine pouches in 2026, you've likely noticed two brands dominating conversations: ZYN, the world's most popular nicotine pouch by a significant margin, and LOOP, the challenger brand with a reputation for bold flavours and cutting-edge nicotine delivery. Both are excellent products—but they're built for different users. Here's everything you need to know to make the right choice.

Brand Backgrounds: The Giants Behind the Pouches

ZYN: The Global Standard

ZYN is made by Swedish Match, a company founded in 1917 and acquired by Philip Morris International in 2022. It's the result of decades of expertise in Swedish oral tobacco products, refined into a tobacco-free, leaf-free nicotine pouch. ZYN launched in 2014 and has become a cultural phenomenon—it owns more than 70% of the US nicotine pouch market and is the best-selling brand in Europe. The brand is backed by PMI's enormous R&D budget, which shows in consistent innovation and quality control.

What makes ZYN special is its pharmaceutical-grade nicotine and tight manufacturing tolerances. Every pouch delivers exactly what the label promises, which matters when you're managing your nicotine intake. Swedish Match has spent a century perfecting oral nicotine delivery; ZYN benefits from that heritage.

LOOP: The Disruptor

LOOP is made by Another Snus Factory (ASF), a Swedish startup founded in 2016. While much younger than ZYN, LOOP has quickly built a cult following by doing things differently. The brand is known for Instant Rush technology—a proprietary formulation that releases nicotine faster than traditional pouches—and for environmental commitment, including plant-based cans made from renewable materials.

LOOP also isn't afraid of flavour adventure. Where ZYN offers mint and citrus, LOOP experiments with jalapeño, habanero, and red chili melon. It's the brand for users who want nicotine delivery to feel like an experience, not just a habit.

Flavour Range: Variety vs. Adventure

ZYN has 51 products across 15+ distinct flavours. You'll find the classics: Cool Mint (the bestseller), Citrus, Apple Mint, Spearmint, and Menthol. But ZYN also offers a dedicated coffee range (Espressini being the standout) and Red Fruits for users who want something fruity. This breadth means most people will find a ZYN they love.

LOOP has 32 products across 10+ flavours, but the distribution is different. Yes, you get Mint (their equivalent to Cool Mint), but LOOP's strength is bold, spicy profiles: Red Chili Melon, Habanero Mint, Jalapeño Lime, and Tropical Mango. If you like your nicotine delivery to tingle, LOOP will excite you. If you're looking for comfort flavours, LOOP offers fewer options.

Verdict: Choose ZYN if you want to try 15+ flavours and find your perfect match. Choose LOOP if you're after bold, spicy, unique tastes that push the category forward.

Strength Range: Who Has More Options?

Nicotine strength is personal. ZYN offers strengths from S1 (Light, 1.5mg) to S6 (Extra Strong, 16.5mg)—six discrete tiers covering everyone from beginners to heavy users. LOOP offers Normal (3-4mg), Strong (8-10mg), Extra Strong (12-13mg), and Hyper Strong (15.6mg)—four main tiers plus a unique option: nicotine-free pouches (0mg). This is brilliant for habit-breaking or behavioural use.

On raw numbers, ZYN's range is wider (1.5–16.5mg vs. 0–15.6mg). But LOOP's inclusion of zero-nicotine options is a genuine differentiator for users trying to quit or reduce dependency.

Verdict: Tie.** ZYN wins on pure range, LOOP wins on inclusivity (zero option). Both go strong enough for any user.

Format & Comfort: Dry or Moist, Small or Full

ZYN comes in Mini (tiny, almost invisible under the lip, dry, 30-45 minute duration) and Slim (standard size, moderate moisture, 45-60 minutes). ZYN pouches are engineered to be dry, which appeals to professionals and users who don't want visible white residue.

LOOP comes in Standard (full-size, moist, traditional feel) and Hyper (even bigger, for maximum flavour release). LOOP pouches have noticeably more moisture, which means faster flavour bloom and faster nicotine release—the Instant Rush advantage. But moisture also means more visible saliva staining if you're concerned about discretion.

Verdict: ZYN for discretion and durability (longer shelf-life, dry storage). LOOP for impact and flavour intensity. Your preference depends on your lifestyle.

Price & Value: Budget Matters

This is where ZYN pulls ahead significantly. With 51 products, ZYN has options across the full price spectrum:

  • Budget ZYN (entry-level strengths, common flavours): €1.22–€2.50 per can
  • Mid-range ZYN (S3–S4, all formats): €2.50–€3.50 per can
  • Premium ZYN (S5–S6, specialty flavours like Espressini): €3.50–€4.88 per can

LOOP, by contrast, keeps prices tightly bunched: €4.17–€4.48 per can, regardless of strength or flavour. This is not cheap—it's 50–200% more expensive than entry-level ZYN—but the premium reflects the Instant Rush technology and plant-based packaging.

If you're budget-conscious, ZYN wins decisively. If you've already decided the experience is worth the price, LOOP's premium is justified.

Verdict: ZYN for value. LOOP for users who've decided premium ingredients and technology are worth the cost.

Innovation & Sustainability: The Future of Pouches

ZYN is backed by Philip Morris International's unlimited R&D budget and has achieved regulatory approval in 70+ countries. The brand is stable and continuously refined, but incremental rather than radical—new strengths and flavours arrive regularly, but the core formula doesn't change dramatically year to year.

LOOP, by contrast, is the innovation leader. Instant Rush technology genuinely does deliver nicotine faster (clinical studies confirm 3–5 minute onset vs. 5–8 minutes for traditional pouches). Plant-based cans reduce environmental impact without compromising function. The brand moves faster and takes more risks.

If climate impact matters to you, LOOP is the clear choice. If you want pharmaceutical-grade stability, ZYN wins.

Verdict: LOOP for innovation and sustainability. ZYN for pharmaceutical reliability.

European Availability

Both brands are widely available across the EU, including Scandinavia, Germany, France, the UK, and Southern Europe. ZYN has a slight edge due to its market dominance and availability in more retail channels. LOOP is carried by most major online retailers and premium tobacconists but has fewer physical retail locations.

Verdict: ZYN by a small margin for convenience in finding stock locally.

Who Should Choose ZYN?

  • Budget-conscious users: Entry-level ZYN cans start at €1.22 and work perfectly for most users. You don't need to spend premium prices.
  • Flavour explorers: With 51 products, you can try 20+ different combinations and still have new flavours to discover.
  • Discretion matters: ZYN Mini is nearly invisible, and the dry pouch format won't stain your teeth or mouth.
  • Beginners: ZYN S1 (1.5mg) is the gentlest entry point. The range covers every step up to Extra Strong.
  • Professionals: Long-lasting, dry, and odourless. ZYN doesn't interfere with your image or meetings.
  • Stability matters: PMI-backed pharmaceutical consistency. Every pouch delivers exactly the promised nicotine.

Who Should Choose LOOP?

  • Flavour adventurers: Habanero Mint, Red Chili Melon, Jalapeño Lime—LOOP doesn't play it safe.
  • Speed seekers: Instant Rush technology gives you nicotine in 3–5 minutes. If you want immediate impact, LOOP delivers.
  • Quitters: LOOP's nicotine-free option is genuinely useful for breaking the habit while keeping the ritual.
  • Environmentalists: Plant-based cans matter if you care about sustainability in every purchase.
  • Enthusiasts: If you're already convinced that premium formulation and technology justify the cost, LOOP is worth it.
  • Moist pouch fans: LOOP's moisture content delivers faster flavour bloom and a more traditional tobacco-like feel.

The Final Verdict

In 2026, ZYN is the better choice for most people. It offers unbeatable versatility (51 products), aggressive pricing (starting at €1.22), and pharmaceutical reliability. If you're new to nicotine pouches or want to explore without commitment, ZYN is the logical starting point. You can find your ideal strength and flavour, then adjust spending as you refine your preferences.

LOOP is the better choice if you've already decided what you want and are willing to pay for it. The Instant Rush technology is genuinely faster, the flavours are genuinely bolder, and the plant-based cans are genuinely better for the planet. LOOP attracts users who've evaluated ZYN and decided they prefer LOOP's approach to nicotine delivery and flavour.

The good news: both brands are excellent, and both are available across Europe. Your decision ultimately comes down to: do you want maximum choice and value (ZYN), or maximum speed and adventure (LOOP)? Start with ZYN if unsure. Switch to LOOP if you want to upgrade the experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is ZYN stronger than LOOP?

No. Both reach 15–16.5mg nicotine, which is "Extra Strong" territory. The difference is speed: LOOP delivers its nicotine faster via Instant Rush technology, so even a LOOP "Normal" (3–4mg) might feel like a stronger hit because it arrives quickly. ZYN S5–S6 delivers more total nicotine, but over a longer period.

Which has more flavours?

ZYN has more flavours (15+) across 51 products. LOOP has fewer flavours (10+) across 32 products. But LOOP's flavours are more adventurous (spicy, bold). ZYN's flavours are more familiar (mint, citrus, coffee).

Which is cheaper?

ZYN is significantly cheaper. Entry-level ZYN starts at €1.22 per can; LOOP's minimum is €4.17. For a heavy user (20 pouches/day), ZYN saves you €50–100 per month.

Can I use both ZYN and LOOP?

Absolutely. Many users rotate between brands for variety or reserve LOOP for special occasions when they want the Instant Rush kick. Both are safe; mixing them won't cause issues. Just track your total daily nicotine intake if you're managing dependence.

Which is better for beginners?

ZYN S1 (1.5mg) is the gentlest introduction to nicotine pouches. It's affordable, available everywhere, and designed for new users. Start with ZYN, then explore stronger options or switch to LOOP if you want to experiment.

Does LOOP really release nicotine faster?

Yes. Clinical studies confirm Instant Rush technology delivers nicotine in 3–5 minutes, compared to 5–8 minutes for traditional pouches like ZYN. The faster onset creates a more pronounced "kick," which some users prefer and others find overwhelming. It's a legitimate differentiator.
