Buying Nicotine Pouches in Finland: A 2026 Guide to Legal Options & Regulations

Finland's nicotine pouch market has undergone dramatic changes since 2025. What was once one of Europe's fastest-growing categories—bolstered by the legal paradox of snus being banned while nicotine pouches were freely available—has now tightened significantly under new tobacco regulations. If you're in Finland and interested in nicotine pouches, understanding the current landscape is essential.

This guide covers what's legal, where you can buy, import rules, popular brands, and practical advice for Finnish consumers in 2026.

The Finnish Nicotine Pouch Paradox: Snus Banned, Pouches Legal (Mostly)

Finland, like all EU member states except Sweden, enforces a complete ban on snus (moist tobacco). This has been in place for decades under EU tobacco law. However, nicotine pouches—tobacco-free white portions containing nicotine salts—were legal to sell and import without the same restrictions that apply to snus.

This legal gap created an opportunity. Between roughly 2020 and mid-2025, nicotine pouches exploded in popularity across Finland. Users who couldn't legally buy snus could purchase pouches in physical shops, order online, and cross-border shop without restriction. The market boomed, particularly among young adults and former snus users from cross-border visits to Sweden.

That changed in 2025.

2025-2026 Regulatory Tightening: What Changed

August 2025: Distance Selling Ban

The Finnish government updated the Tupakkalaki (Tobacco Act), implementing new restrictions on nicotine pouches. The most significant change: online sales to consumers in Finland are now prohibited. This is known as the distance selling ban, enforced by Valvira, Finland's National Supervisory Authority for Welfare and Health (Kansanterveyslaitos).

What this means:

  • International online shops (including SnusFriend) cannot legally ship nicotine pouches to addresses in Finland.
  • Finnish online retailers cannot sell nicotine pouches to consumers remotely.
  • Custom enforcement actively blocks shipments of nicotine pouches destined for Finnish consumers.

Flavor Restrictions

Only mint and menthol flavors are permitted. Characterizing flavors (fruit, candy, spice, etc.) are banned under EU tobacco harmonization rules that Finland has adopted. This affects brand variety: products like LOOP Xtra Cool Berry or other fruit variants are no longer sold in Finland.

Nicotine Strength Cap

Maximum nicotine content is limited to 16.6 mg per gram of product. Most mainstream brands comply with this, but some ultra-strong products are unavailable in Finnish retail.

Age & ID Requirements

Buyers must be 18+. Physical retailers check ID consistently, particularly larger chains like R-kioski and supermarkets.

How Finns Actually Buy Nicotine Pouches in 2026

Option 1: Physical Retail in Finland (Legal)

This is now the primary route for Finnish consumers. Nicotine pouches remain legal to *purchase* in physical shops; the ban applies to distance selling, not retail stores.

Major retailers stocking nicotine pouches:

  • R-kioski — Finland's largest kiosk chain (~400 locations). Reliable stock of VELO, ZYN, and occasionally LOOP. Price: €5–€7 per can.
  • K-Market — Major supermarket chain. Similar brands and pricing.
  • S-Market — Another large supermarket with consistent stock in larger stores.
  • Prisma — Discount supermarket chain, occasional good pricing on multi-packs.

Selection is typically limited to the most popular compliant products (mint/menthol flavors only). Don't expect variety; expect 2–4 VELO options, maybe ZYN, and occasionally LOOP.

Option 2: Personal Import from EU (Legal)

As of February 2026, Finnish consumers can legally import nicotine pouches for personal use from other EU countries, provided they:

  • Are traveling in person (or having goods shipped to a non-Finnish EU address).
  • Import up to 1 kg per day for personal consumption.
  • Comply with the product standards of the country of origin (mint/menthol, ≤16.6mg/g).

This has created a genuine shopping corridor in the Nordic region. The Helsinki-Tallinn ferry (30-minute crossing) is now a well-known route for nicotine pouch shopping. Ferry passengers regularly visit Tallinn's shops (e.g., small convenience stores, pharmacies) and return with supplies. The same applies to trips to Stockholm, Germany, or Poland.

How to Use Personal Import:

If traveling to another EU country, you can purchase nicotine pouches locally or arrange shipment to a hotel, Airbnb, or click-and-collect point in that country. SnusFriend and other EU retailers can ship to Estonia, Sweden, Germany, and other EU member states if you provide a non-Finnish address. Many Finnish travelers now use this strategy during planned trips.

Option 3: Cross-Border Shopping (Practical)

Taking advantage of the ferry routes and border proximity:

  • Tallinn — Quick ferry from Helsinki. Local shops stock nicotine pouches cheaply (often cheaper than Finland).
  • Stockholm — Domestic flights or longer journey. Systembolaget (state monopoly) and supermarkets stock pouches.
  • Aland Islands — Technically Finnish but with different tax status; ferry accessible but enforcement is unclear.

Note: Bringing back personal imports must stay within the 1kg/day allowance and be for personal use only (not resale).

Most Popular Brands & Flavors in Finland

Finnish preferences lean toward strong nicotine and mint/menthol flavors, reflecting the influence of snus culture in neighboring Sweden and cross-border shopping habits. Here are the dominant brands:

1. VELO

Most widely available in Finnish R-kioski locations. Reliable, consistent product. VELO Freeze is the most common flavor in Finland. Strong brand recognition due to historical availability.

2. ZYN

Premium brand positioning, strong globally. ZYN Cool Mint 6mg and ZYN Slim are popular entry points; ZYN Citrus Freeze is unavailable in Finland but Swedish travelers bring it back. Slightly higher price than VELO (€6–€7).

3. LOOP

Was extremely popular before the 2025 flavor ban eliminated many products from Finnish retail. LOOP Mint Mania remains available if imported; Finnish stores rarely stock it due to flavor restrictions on other LOOP variants.

4. Skruf

Scandinavian brand with strong heritage. Skruf Fresh Mint is available in some Finnish stores. Less common than VELO or ZYN but trusted by Nordic consumers.

5. White Fox

Premium strong option. Growing presence in Scandinavian markets. Particularly popular among users transitioning from Swedish snus.

Flavor Availability:

Mint and menthol dominate. Expect Freeze, Cool Mint, Fresh Mint, and similar variants. Do not expect fruit, spice, or candy flavors in Finnish retail.

Strength Preferences:

Finnish users tend toward medium-to-strong nicotine. Expect 11–16mg to be more popular than 3–6mg (unlike some other EU markets where lighter strengths dominate).

Pricing & Availability Across Finland's Regions

Helsinki and major cities have better selection than rural areas. R-kioski serves as a reliable fallback nationwide—if you need a pouch, your local R-kioski likely has at least VELO Freeze.

Typical prices:

  • VELO: €5–€6 per can
  • ZYN: €6–€7 per can
  • Multi-packs (10 cans): €50–€60 at Prisma or S-Market

Prices are significantly higher than in Estonia or Sweden (where the same products cost €3–€4 per can), which fuels the cross-border shopping behavior.

The Distance Selling Ban: Why SnusFriend Can't Ship to Finland

International retailers like SnusFriend cannot legally deliver nicotine pouches to Finland. This is enforced by Finnish customs and Valvira. Orders destined for Finnish addresses are actively blocked at the border.

This doesn't reflect a choice by retailers—it's a legal requirement. Attempting to circumvent this (shipping to a Finnish address via a forwarding service, for example) violates Finnish tobacco law.

What You Can Do Instead:

If you're determined to access a wider selection, order to a non-Finnish EU address (hotel, friend, Airbnb in Estonia, Sweden, etc.) and arrange personal collection during travel. This remains fully legal and is increasingly common among Finnish consumers seeking better prices and flavor variety.

Recommendations for Beginners in Finland

If you're new to nicotine pouches and live in Finland, start with these widely available options from R-kioski or supermarkets:

  1. ZYN Cool Mint 6mg — Gentle introduction. Mild nicotine, clean mint flavor. Perfect for first-time users.
  2. VELO Freeze 11mg — Finnish favorite. Stronger, familiar to locals. Reliable kick without being overwhelming.
  3. Skruf Fresh Mint #2 — Balanced middle ground. 10–11mg range, fresh mint without menthol chill.
  4. ZYN Slim Mint 6mg — Slimmer format if you prefer less bulk. Same nicotine as the standard 6mg.

Avoid jumping straight to ultra-strong products (15–16mg) unless you've used snus or nicotine regularly. Most Finnish users escalate to stronger options within weeks, but starting moderate allows you to gauge your tolerance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are nicotine pouches legal in Finland?

Yes, but with restrictions. You can legally buy and use nicotine pouches in Finland, but only from physical retailers. Online/distance sales are banned since August 2025. Only mint and menthol flavors are permitted.

Can I order nicotine pouches online to Finland?

No. International and domestic online retailers cannot ship to Finnish addresses. This is actively enforced by customs. The only workaround is to order to a non-Finnish EU address and collect in person while traveling.

How much can I bring back from Estonia or Sweden?

Up to 1 kg per day for personal use. This is the legal personal import allowance under current EU and Finnish regulations (as of February 2026). Anything beyond that may be deemed commercial import, which is restricted.

Why is snus banned but nicotine pouches allowed in Finland?

Snus is a tobacco product and falls under the EU-wide snus ban (Sweden has an exemption; Finland does not). Nicotine pouches are tobacco-free and were initially not covered by snus restrictions, creating a legal gap. That gap is now narrowed: pouches are allowed but distance sales are banned.

What brands are actually available in Finnish shops?

Primarily VELO, ZYN, and occasionally LOOP. Selection is limited. Smaller chains may stock Skruf or White Fox. Expect 2–4 mint/menthol variants per brand, nothing more.

What flavors can I legally buy in Finland?

Mint and menthol only. Fruit, spice, candy, and tobacco flavors are banned. This applies to all products sold in Finnish retail and imported for personal use.

How do Finnish prices compare to other EU countries?

Finland has the highest retail prices in the Nordic region. A can that costs €3–€4 in Estonia or Sweden costs €5–€7 in Finland. This is due to higher taxes and a smaller market. This price differential is the primary reason Finns cross-border shop.

Summary: Your Options in 2026

Best for convenience: R-kioski. Reliable stock, nationwide presence, no planning required.

Best for selection & price: Cross-border shopping in Estonia (ferry) or Sweden. Requires planning but significantly cheaper and offers more flavors.

Not an option: Online ordering to Finland. Accept this and plan accordingly.

Finland's regulatory environment has tightened, but nicotine pouches remain legal and accessible. The shift from "buy online whenever" to "physical retail only" is real, but for most users, visiting a local R-kioski remains simple and hassle-free. For those seeking better prices or variety, the nearby Nordic countries are just a ferry ride away.
