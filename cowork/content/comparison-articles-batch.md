### The Two Contenders

VELO and ON! are both backed by tobacco industry giants — VELO by British American Tobacco, ON! by Philip Morris International (through its Helix Innovations subsidiary). Both brands aim squarely at the mainstream nicotine pouch consumer, but they take very different approaches to format, strength range, and flavour philosophy.

This guide compares them across every dimension that matters to a buyer: flavour variety, nicotine delivery, pouch comfort, price, and availability across Europe.

### Brand Backgrounds

**VELO** launched as BAT's global answer to ZYN. Rebranded from the earlier "Lyft" name in several European markets, VELO has grown into the second-largest nicotine pouch brand in Europe by volume. BAT has invested heavily in flavour innovation, with VELO offering one of the widest product ranges in the market — over 50 SKUs spanning five flavour families.

**ON!** takes a different path. Originally developed by Burger Söhne in Switzerland and later acquired by Altria (then distributed under PMI's umbrella), ON! focuses on miniature pouches designed for maximum discretion. The brand made global headlines in 2025 when on! PLUS became the first nicotine pouch to receive FDA marketing authorisation in the United States — a significant regulatory milestone for the entire category.

### Head-to-Head: Flavour Range

VELO dominates in flavour breadth. The European range spans mint (peppermint, spearmint, wintergreen), berry (mixed berry, raspberry, elderflower), citrus (orange, lime, grapefruit), fruit (tropical, mango), and licorice. New releases appear quarterly, and regional exclusives keep the lineup fresh.

ON! takes a focused approach. The core range covers approximately 10–12 flavours: Mint, Wintergreen, Berry, Citrus, Coffee, Cinnamon, and a few regional variants. ON! does not chase novelty — the emphasis is on consistency and a tighter flavour portfolio where every variant has been rigorously tested.

**Verdict:** VELO wins for variety seekers. ON! wins for users who want a reliable favourite without decision fatigue.

### Head-to-Head: Strength and Nicotine Delivery

VELO offers a wide strength ladder: from 4 mg (Bright/Easy line) up to 17 mg (Max/Ultra line in select markets). The range covers beginners through to experienced users looking for a serious nicotine hit. VELO's moisture level is relatively high, which means faster initial flavour and nicotine release — the hit comes within 60–90 seconds.

ON! is built around the mini format, which means each pouch is physically smaller. Strengths range from 2 mg to 8 mg per pouch in most European markets. The lower absolute nicotine content per pouch is partly offset by ON!'s higher bioavailability — the mini format sits closer to the gum, and ON! uses a pH-optimised formulation that improves absorption efficiency.

**Key difference:** VELO delivers higher peak nicotine per pouch. ON! delivers a gentler, more sustained release. Heavy users may find they need more ON! pouches per day to match a VELO habit.

**Verdict:** VELO for users who want a stronger per-pouch experience. ON! for users who prefer micro-dosing or very discreet use.

### Head-to-Head: Format and Comfort

VELO offers both slim and mini formats. The slim is the most popular — it sits flat under the lip, is comfortable for 25–35 minutes, and is available across all flavours and strengths. The VELO mini is smaller but still noticeably larger than an ON! mini.

ON! is almost exclusively a mini-format brand. The pouches are among the smallest on the market — roughly the size of a thumbnail. This makes them virtually invisible under the lip, ideal for situations where discretion is paramount (office meetings, public transport, social settings).

The trade-off is session duration: ON! minis typically last 15–25 minutes before flavour fades, compared to 25–35 minutes for VELO slims. Some ON! users cycle through pouches faster as a result.

**Verdict:** ON! for discretion. VELO for comfort and session length.

### Head-to-Head: Price and Value

In most European markets, VELO and ON! are priced competitively. VELO cans typically contain 20 pouches at €3.50–5.50 per can. ON! cans also contain 20 pouches, priced at €3.00–5.00 per can.

On a per-milligram basis, VELO often delivers more total nicotine per can (because higher-strength options are available), making it better value for users who measure by nicotine content rather than pouch count.

**Verdict:** Roughly even on price. VELO edges ahead on per-mg value at higher strengths.

### Head-to-Head: European Availability

VELO has broader European distribution. It is available in the UK, Germany, Poland, Austria, Scandinavia, and most Western European markets. BAT's retail partnerships and marketing spend give VELO strong shelf presence.

ON! is expanding but still has gaps. It is well-established in the UK, US, and select Nordic markets, but availability in Southern and Eastern Europe is patchier. Online retailers like SnusFriend stock the full ON! range regardless of your location.

**Verdict:** VELO for in-store availability. Both brands are fully available online through SnusFriend.

### The FDA Factor

ON! has a unique selling point that no other European brand can match: the on! PLUS range received FDA marketing authorisation in the US. While this does not directly affect European sales, it signals a level of regulatory scrutiny and product safety data that competitors have not yet achieved. For health-conscious consumers, this is a meaningful trust signal.

VELO has not pursued FDA authorisation but is subject to UK and EU consumer product regulations. BAT conducts extensive internal testing, though the results are not independently verified to the same regulatory standard as FDA review.

### Who Should Choose VELO?

VELO is the right choice if you want the widest flavour selection available, prefer stronger pouches (8–17 mg range), value longer session duration (25–35 min), or want a brand with broad in-store availability across Europe.

### Who Should Choose ON!?

ON! is the right choice if you prioritise discretion above all else (the smallest pouches on the market), prefer lighter nicotine doses (2–8 mg range), value the FDA-authorised safety data behind on! PLUS, or want a simple, focused product range without overwhelming choice.

### Final Verdict

There is no wrong choice between VELO and ON! — the right brand depends on your priorities. VELO is the broader, bolder option with more flavours and higher strengths. ON! is the specialist for discreet, lighter use backed by the strongest regulatory credentials in the category.

Many users keep both on rotation: VELO for when you want flavour and intensity, ON! for when you need to be invisible.

### Two Swedish Powerhouses

ZYN and Nordic Spirit are both premium Swedish nicotine pouch brands, but they appeal to different audiences. ZYN — the world's best-selling nicotine pouch — is known for its clean taste and global reach. Nordic Spirit — owned by Japan Tobacco International — has built a loyal following with its distinctive cylindrical can, muted design language, and consistent, understated flavour profiles.

This comparison helps you decide which belongs in your rotation.

### Brand Heritage

**ZYN** was created by Swedish Match, now a Philip Morris International company. ZYN pioneered the modern nicotine pouch category and remains the market leader by a significant margin. In the US alone, ZYN shipped over 500 million cans in 2025. The European range, while smaller than the US lineup, covers the core flavours and strengths that built the brand.

**Nordic Spirit** was launched by JTI (Japan Tobacco International) as a direct competitor to ZYN in the European market. The brand's Scandinavian design ethos — clean lines, earthy colour palette, minimal branding — positions it as the sophisticated alternative. Nordic Spirit has been particularly successful in the UK, where it is one of the top-selling nicotine pouch brands.

### Head-to-Head: Flavour Range

ZYN's European range includes approximately 15–20 flavours spanning mint, citrus, coffee, and berry categories. The core lineup — Cool Mint, Citrus, Espressino, Bellini — is tight and well-curated. ZYN flavours tend toward clean, subtle profiles that do not overpower. The approach is minimalist: every flavour in the range is designed to be inoffensive enough for all-day use.

Nordic Spirit offers around 10–12 flavours. Spearmint, Frosty Mint, Bergamot Wildberry, Elderflower, and Mocha are the headliners. Nordic Spirit's flavour philosophy sits between ZYN's subtlety and VELO's boldness — there is enough character to be interesting, but the profiles remain smooth and composed. Bergamot Wildberry is a standout: a sophisticated berry-floral blend that does not exist elsewhere in the market.

**Verdict:** ZYN for a wider selection. Nordic Spirit for more distinctive, nuanced flavour profiles.

### Head-to-Head: Strength and Delivery

ZYN's European range spans 1.5 mg (mini) to 11 mg (strong slim). The strength ladder is well-spaced: beginners start at 1.5–3 mg, regulars settle at 6 mg, and strong users use 9.5–11 mg. ZYN's nicotine release is gradual and even — no sharp spikes, which makes it comfortable for extended sessions.

Nordic Spirit offers strengths from 6 mg to 11 mg in most European markets, with some variants reaching 13 mg. The entry point is higher than ZYN's, which means Nordic Spirit is less suitable for complete beginners. Nicotine delivery is slightly faster than ZYN — Nordic Spirit pouches are moister out of the can, which accelerates initial absorption.

**Verdict:** ZYN for beginners and users who want a gentle ramp-up. Nordic Spirit for regular-to-strong users who prefer a quicker onset.

### Head-to-Head: Format and Pouch Feel

Both brands offer slim-format pouches. ZYN's slim is dry and flat — it moulds to the gum with almost no mouth feel after a few minutes. Some users love this (invisible, comfortable); others find the initial dryness requires a minute to activate.

Nordic Spirit's pouches are slightly moister and softer. They activate faster and produce more flavour in the first 2–3 minutes. The trade-off is that some users find them slightly bulkier. Nordic Spirit also offers a mini format in select flavours.

**Verdict:** Tie — personal preference. Dry and invisible (ZYN) vs moist and fast-activating (Nordic Spirit).

### Head-to-Head: Packaging

ZYN uses a traditional flat, round can — the standard in the industry. Clean design, brand colour coding by flavour, easy to pocket.

Nordic Spirit uses a distinctive tall, cylindrical can that stands out on a shelf. The can is narrower but taller, which some users prefer for pocket carry. The design is deliberately premium — matte finish, muted earth tones, minimal text. Nordic Spirit's packaging is arguably the best-looking in the category.

**Verdict:** Nordic Spirit for design and shelf appeal. ZYN for conventional pocketability.

### Head-to-Head: Price

In most European markets, ZYN and Nordic Spirit are priced within €0.50 of each other per can. Nordic Spirit occasionally runs slightly cheaper in the UK (where JTI has strong distribution deals), while ZYN may be cheaper in Scandinavian markets where Swedish Match has home advantage.

**Verdict:** Essentially tied. Neither brand commands a meaningful price premium over the other.

### Head-to-Head: Availability

ZYN has the widest distribution of any nicotine pouch brand — available in 20+ European countries, the US, and growing in Asia. If a shop sells nicotine pouches, it almost certainly sells ZYN.

Nordic Spirit is strongest in the UK, Germany, and select Scandinavian markets. It has grown rapidly but still has narrower distribution than ZYN, particularly in Southern and Eastern Europe. Online availability through SnusFriend covers the full range regardless of your country.

**Verdict:** ZYN for guaranteed availability anywhere. Nordic Spirit is easy to find in Western Europe but patchier elsewhere.

### Who Should Choose ZYN?

ZYN suits you if you want the safe, universal choice that is available everywhere, prefer subtle flavours for all-day use, need lower strength options (1.5–3 mg) for light use or beginners, or want the brand with the longest track record and largest user base.

### Who Should Choose Nordic Spirit?

Nordic Spirit suits you if you appreciate distinctive, nuanced flavours (Bergamot Wildberry, Elderflower), prefer moister pouches with faster activation, value premium packaging and design, are based in the UK (where Nordic Spirit has the strongest retail presence), or want a brand that feels less mainstream than ZYN.

### Side-by-Side Comparison Table

| Feature | ZYN | Nordic Spirit |
|---------|-----|---------------|
| Maker | Swedish Match (PMI) | JTI |
| Origin | Sweden | Sweden |
| Formats | Slim + Mini | Slim + Mini |
| Strength range | 1.5–11 mg | 6–13 mg |
| Flavour count (EU) | 15–20 | 10–12 |
| Pouch feel | Dry, flat, invisible | Moist, soft, fast-activating |
| Can design | Traditional round | Tall cylindrical (premium) |
| Price range (EU) | €3.50–5.50 | €3.50–5.00 |
| Best market | Global | UK, Germany |
| Best for | Universality, light strengths | Nuanced flavours, premium feel |

### Final Verdict

ZYN and Nordic Spirit are both excellent choices — you will not be disappointed with either. ZYN is the safe pick: universally available, consistently good, with the widest strength range. Nordic Spirit is the connoisseur's pick: better packaging, more interesting flavours, and a premium feel that ZYN's utilitarian design does not match.

The smartest move? Try one can of each in the same flavour family (e.g., ZYN Cool Mint vs Nordic Spirit Frosty Mint) and let your palate decide.
