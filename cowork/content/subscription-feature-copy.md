## Subscription FAQ (for PDP accordion or dedicated section)

### Q: How does the SnusFriend subscription work?

Pick your product and choose a delivery frequency (monthly or every 6 months). Your first order ships right away. Before each renewal, we'll email you a heads-up 3 days in advance so there are no surprises. You'll earn double SnusPoints on every subscription delivery, and shipping is always free.

### Q: Can I change, pause, or cancel my subscription?

Yes — at any time, no questions asked. Log into your account, go to "My Subscriptions," and you can skip your next delivery, change the product or frequency, or cancel entirely. If you cancel, you keep any remaining SnusPoints you've earned. There are no cancellation fees or hidden charges.

### Q: What if a product I subscribe to goes out of stock?

We'll notify you by email and pause that delivery automatically — you won't be charged. Once the product is back in stock, your subscription resumes on the next scheduled date. If you'd prefer to swap to a different product instead of waiting, you can do that from your account page.
