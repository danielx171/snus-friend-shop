## Implementation Notes

- Use natural, descriptive anchor text — avoid "click here" or "read more"
- Place links within the first 2–3 paragraphs of relevant sections (above the fold)
- Ensure each link opens in the same tab (no `target="_blank"` for internal links)
- After implementation, verify no broken links with `bun run build`
- Re-submit updated sitemap to GSC after deployment
