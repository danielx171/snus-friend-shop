Legal Status & Regulatory Landscape (2026)

Nicotine pouches are fully legal and widely available in Poland. However, new regulations in 2025–2026 have reshaped the market:

Excise Tax: The 2026 Game Changer

Poland introduced an excise tax on nicotine pouches in April 2025, implemented via the Tobacco Products Directive (2014/40/EU). The rates are:

  • 2026: 200 PLN per kilogram of nicotine content (~€48)
  • 2027: 280 PLN per kg (~€67)
  • 2028: 360 PLN per kg (~€86)

For a typical 20mg/gram strength pouch can, this adds roughly 0.50–1.20 PLN to retail price, depending on nicotine content. Budget brands remain under 20 PLN per can; premium brands have crossed 25 PLN in some Żabka locations.

Product Standards

  • Max nicotine: 20mg per gram (EU-wide limit, enforced by Główny Inspektorat Sanitarny—Chief Sanitary Inspectorate)
  • Banned additives: Vitamins, caffeine, taurine, and other substances implying health benefits (EU Regulation 2023/5)
  • Age restriction: 18+ (enforced at point of sale; online sellers must verify age)
  • Registration: All manufacturers and importers must register with Polish tax authorities (Ministerstwo Zdrowia) before selling
