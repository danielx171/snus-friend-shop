Why ZYN Dominates

ZYN isn't just the best-selling nicotine pouch brand in Europe — it's the one that brought pouches into the mainstream. Swedish Match (now part of Philip Morris International) built ZYN around a simple formula: consistent nicotine delivery, clean flavour profiles, and a slim format that disappears under your lip. In 2025, the US FDA authorised 20 ZYN products across 10 flavour profiles, making it the first nicotine pouch brand with that distinction. The European lineup is broader still, with exclusive flavours, higher strengths, and limited-edition releases that rotate through the catalogue.

This guide covers every ZYN flavour currently available, ranked by flavour quality, nicotine delivery, and how long the experience lasts. If you're new to ZYN, start with the top five. If you already have a favourite, skip to the full list and see where it lands.

How We Ranked

Every flavour was evaluated on three criteria. Flavour accuracy: does it taste like what it claims to be? A citrus pouch should deliver actual citrus, not a vague sweetness. Longevity: how long does the flavour hold before it fades? Anything under 20 minutes is a problem. Nicotine delivery: is the hit smooth and predictable, or does it spike and crash? We also factored in community feedback from reviews on SnusFriend.

The Top 10 ZYN Flavours

1. Apple Mint

Apple Mint has quietly become the most popular ZYN flavour in Europe, and it earns the top spot here. The apple is crisp and natural — think Granny Smith, not candy — with a cool mint finish that kicks in around the 5-minute mark. Available in slim format at 6 mg and 9 mg. Flavour holds for 35+ minutes with no bitter tail. If you only try one ZYN, make it this one.

2. Cool Mint — 6 mg

The classic. Cool Mint is ZYN's flagship and the flavour most people start with. Spearmint-forward with a moderate cooling effect — it's refreshing without being aggressive. At 6 mg, the nicotine delivery is smooth and predictable. Flavour consistency is exceptional: the first minute tastes the same as the thirtieth. It's the benchmark every other mint pouch is measured against.

3. Citrus — 6 mg

Citrus is ZYN's other pillar, and it's surprisingly good for what sounds like a generic flavour name. It leans bergamot and lemon zest rather than orange juice — more sophisticated than expected. The nicotine delivery matches Cool Mint, and the flavour longevity is comparable. Works well as an all-day rotation partner alongside a mint variant.

4. Spearmint — 3 mg

The lighter strength option that doesn't sacrifice flavour. Spearmint is gentler than Cool Mint — less menthol chill, more herbal sweetness. At 3 mg, it's ideal for morning use or for anyone transitioning down from higher strengths. The slim pouch sits flat and comfortable. Flavour fades more gradually than Cool Mint but peaks lower.

5. Menthol Ice Super Strong

One of ZYN's newer releases and a serious step up in intensity. The menthol is clinical-strength — this will numb your gums for the first two minutes, then settle into a sustained cold burn. At super strong nicotine levels, this is for experienced users only. Not subtle. Not trying to be. Flavour holds for 40+ minutes because the menthol compound is slow-release.

6. Black Cherry Strong

A pleasant surprise in the ZYN lineup. Black Cherry delivers a dark, tart fruitiness that avoids the artificial candy trap. The "strong" label puts it at a nicotine level that most regular users will find satisfying without being overwhelming. The cherry flavour is front-loaded — strongest in the first 10 minutes, then gradually gives way to a mildly sweet base. Solid pick for fruit-flavour fans who find berry blends too sweet.

7. Coffee — 6 mg

ZYN Coffee is a love-it-or-leave-it flavour. When it works, it delivers a genuine espresso character — roasted, slightly bitter, with no added sweetness. It pairs absurdly well with actual coffee. The downside: the flavour is more subtle than the mint or citrus offerings, and some users find it too mild after minute 20. At 6 mg, the nicotine delivery is standard ZYN.

8. Peppermint — 6 mg

Peppermint sits between Cool Mint and Menthol on the intensity scale. More herbal than Cool Mint, less menthol than Menthol. It's a middle-ground flavour that appeals to users who find Cool Mint too familiar but Menthol Ice too extreme. Solid all-rounder. Flavour longevity is above average at 30+ minutes.

9. Black Currant Ice Super Strong

The European exclusive that's been generating buzz since late 2025. Black currant is a bold flavour choice — tart, dark berry with a tannic edge — and the ice compound adds a cooling layer that balances the tartness. Super strong nicotine means this isn't for beginners, but for experienced users who want something genuinely different from mint, it's one of the most interesting ZYN releases in years.

10. Red Fruits Strong

A blended berry flavour that sits in the middle of the pack. Red Fruits covers strawberry, raspberry, and cranberry territory without committing to any single one. It's pleasant and easy to use all day, but it lacks the sharpness that makes the top-five flavours memorable. Good for variety rotation, less good as a daily driver.

The After Dark Series

ZYN's limited-edition After Dark range launched in 2025 with three cocktail-inspired flavours: Mojito (lime and mint — the best of the three), Espresso (espresso martini with coffee and cocoa notes), and Spiced Cider (warm cinnamon-apple). These rotate in and out of stock, so grab them when they're available. The Mojito variant is genuinely excellent — it takes the Cool Mint base and adds a natural lime brightness that lifts the whole experience.

ZYN Strengths Explained

ZYN uses a straightforward strength system. In the US, options are 3 mg and 6 mg (the FDA-authorised variants). In Europe, the range extends from 1.5 mg up to 12+ mg. Here's a rough guide:

  • 1.5–3 mg: Light. Good for beginners, all-day use, or stepping down from higher strengths.
  • 6 mg: Regular. The sweet spot for most daily users. Noticeable hit without discomfort.
  • 9–11 mg: Strong. Solid buzz. Better for experienced users or people switching from cigarettes.
  • 12+ mg: Extra Strong / Super Strong. Significant nicotine kick. Only for users with established tolerance.

How ZYN Compares

ZYN's main competitors in Europe are VELO, White Fox, and Skruf. Each has strengths: VELO offers a wider flavour range (30+ variants), White Fox delivers more extreme cooling effects, and Skruf has a more subtle, tea-like approach to flavour. ZYN wins on consistency — the flavour and nicotine delivery are predictable from can to can, which matters when you're going through a pouch every hour.

Where ZYN loses: it's generally not the cheapest option per can, and the higher-strength European variants aren't always easy to find. If price matters, check our budget pouch guide.
