VELO: Europe's Biggest Nicotine Pouch Brand

VELO is BAT's (British American Tobacco) nicotine pouch brand, and it holds the largest market share in Europe. Where ZYN wins on consistency and simplicity, VELO wins on variety — with over 30 flavours across a range of strengths from 2 mg up to 17 mg. The brand has gone through several evolutions: the original VELO lineup, the VELO X-Freeze ultra-cooling variants, and the newest VELO Shift collection that launched in autumn 2025 with an eco-friendlier can design and revamped pouch format.

This guide ranks every current VELO flavour based on taste quality, nicotine delivery, and longevity. If you've only tried one or two VELO variants, this will help you figure out what to try next.

How We Ranked

Same methodology as our ZYN ranking: flavour accuracy (does it taste like what it claims?), longevity (does it hold past 20 minutes?), and nicotine delivery (smooth and predictable vs. spiky). We also weighted novelty — VELO's range is so large that the best flavours are the ones that do something genuinely different from what you can get elsewhere.

Top 10 VELO Flavours

1. Freezing Peppermint — 10 mg

VELO's most intense mint offering and our top pick for the brand. "Freezing" is not an exaggeration — the cooling effect is immediate and aggressive, hitting within 30 seconds of placement. The peppermint flavour underneath is clean and sharp, not sweet. At 10 mg, the nicotine delivery is strong but controlled. Flavour lasts 40+ minutes. If you like your mint extreme, this is the benchmark.

2. Ruby Berry — 6 mg

The best berry-flavoured pouch on the market, full stop. Ruby Berry delivers a mix of cranberry and pomegranate — tart, slightly dry, with no artificial candy sweetness. It's sophisticated in a way that most berry flavours aren't. At 6 mg, it works as an all-day option. Flavour peaks around minute 5 and holds steady for 30 minutes. This is the flavour we recommend to people who think they don't like fruit pouches.

3. Icy Berries — 10 mg

Where Ruby Berry is subtle, Icy Berries is bold. It combines mixed berries with a cooling menthol layer that bridges the gap between fruit and mint. The berry flavour is broader here — strawberry, blueberry, and blackcurrant all show up. The 10 mg strength means this hits hard, and the cooling effect gives it an extra dimension that pure fruit pouches lack. Great for users rotating between mint and fruit.

4. Bright Spearmint — 6 mg

VELO's answer to ZYN Cool Mint. Bright Spearmint is gentler than Freezing Peppermint — herbal and sweet rather than icy. It's the kind of mint flavour you can use all day without fatigue. At 6 mg, it's the ideal daily driver for users who want mint without intensity. Flavour longevity is solid at 25–30 minutes, though it does fade more than the Freezing variant.

5. Zesty Elderflower — 6 mg

One of the most unique flavours in any brand's catalogue. Elderflower is floral, slightly honeyed, with a citrus brightness that lifts it above novelty status. It's the kind of flavour you either love immediately or dismiss — there's no middle ground. At 6 mg, the nicotine is moderate and the flavour does most of the talking. If you're bored with mint and berry rotations, this is the pattern-breaker.

6. Mighty Peppermint Max — 17 mg

The strongest VELO in the European range. At 17 mg, this is strictly for users with high nicotine tolerance — the kind of strength that makes extra strong ZYN variants feel light. The peppermint flavour is the same clean profile as Freezing Peppermint, but the nicotine dominates the experience. Use with caution. Definitely not for beginners.

7. Tropical Mango — 6 mg

VELO's mango flavour is better than it has any right to be. Most mango-flavoured nicotine products taste like air freshener — this one manages to deliver a ripe, sweet mango that actually resembles the fruit. The flavour is front-loaded (strongest in the first 10 minutes) and fades to a pleasant sweetness. Pairs well with warm weather. At 6 mg, it's an easy, enjoyable pouch.

8. Icy Cherry — 10 mg

Cherry with a menthol finish. The cherry is more Morello than Maraschino — tart and dry rather than sweet and artificial. The ice compound adds a cooling layer that prevents the fruitiness from becoming cloying. At 10 mg, this is a strong option. Flavour holds for about 25 minutes. Good for users who want something different from the mint-berry axis.

9. Frosty Lemon — 6 mg

A citrus option that leans sharp and clean. Frosty Lemon delivers lemon zest with a cooling finish — think iced lemonade rather than lemon candy. It's refreshing and works well as a morning pouch. At 6 mg, nicotine delivery is standard. The flavour is lighter than most VELO offerings, which some users will love and others will find too subtle.

10. Orange Spark — 6 mg

A warmer citrus alternative to Frosty Lemon. Orange Spark is sweeter and rounder — blood orange with a hint of mandarin. No cooling effect, which makes it stand out in a lineup that leans heavily on ice compounds. The flavour is pleasant and uncomplicated. At 6 mg, it's an easygoing all-day option for citrus fans who don't want the cold.

The VELO Shift Collection

Launched in autumn 2025, VELO Shift is a reformulated line with a new slim pouch design and an eco-friendlier can (reduced plastic). The flavours emphasise "heating or cooling effects" — a departure from the standard VELO range. The Shift collection is rolling out across European markets, so availability varies by country. Early standouts include Shift Spearmint (similar to Bright Spearmint but with a new pouch material that releases flavour faster) and Shift Berry Frost (a bolder take on the Icy Berries profile).

VELO Strength Guide

VELO uses a dot system on the can (2 dots through 5 dots) alongside mg values. Here's the mapping:

  • 2–4 mg (●●): Light. Entry-level, good for beginners or all-day use.
  • 6 mg (●●●): Medium. The most popular strength tier. Balanced nicotine and flavour.
  • 10 mg (●●●●): Strong. Noticeable buzz. For regular users.
  • 14–17 mg (●●●●●): Extra Strong / Max. Serious nicotine delivery. Experienced users only.

If you're coming from ZYN, a VELO 6 mg is roughly comparable to a ZYN 6 mg in perceived strength. VELO's higher-strength offerings (14–17 mg) go beyond what ZYN currently offers in Europe, which is one reason VELO has a strong following among heavy users.

VELO vs. ZYN: Which Is Better?

It depends on what you value. ZYN is more consistent can-to-can, has a tighter flavour range (focused on doing fewer things well), and has FDA authorisation in the US. VELO offers more variety, goes to higher strengths, and tends to have bolder flavour profiles — particularly in the fruit and cooling categories. The pouch format is similar (both slim), though many users find VELO's newer pouch material slightly softer.
