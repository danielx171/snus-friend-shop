### Section A: Testing Methodology (~200 words)

**Insert after the main bio paragraph:**

#### How Erik Tests Nicotine Pouches

Every product review on SnusFriend follows a standardised testing protocol developed over 200+ product evaluations. Erik tests each pouch across five dimensions:

**1. Flavour accuracy and quality** — Does the pouch deliver on its flavour promise? Is the taste natural or artificial? How does it compare to the category leader? Scored 1–10.

**2. Nicotine delivery consistency** — Is the nicotine release smooth and predictable, or does it spike and crash? Tested across multiple pouches from the same can to check batch consistency. Scored 1–10.

**3. Pouch construction and moisture** — Is the pouch well-sealed? Does it maintain its shape under the lip? Is the moisture level appropriate for the format (slim, mini, dry)? Scored 1–10.

**4. Duration of effect** — How long does the flavour last? How long does the nicotine effect persist after removal? Timed across three sessions per product. Scored 1–10.

**5. Overall value for money** — Price per pouch relative to the quality delivered. Compared against category benchmarks (budget, mid-range, premium). Scored 1–10.

All testing is blind where possible — cans are decanted by a colleague into unmarked containers for flavour and quality scoring. Brand identity is revealed only after scores are finalised. No brand has editorial approval or score modification rights.
