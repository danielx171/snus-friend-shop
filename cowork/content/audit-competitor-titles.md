## SnusFriend Strategic Recommendations

### Title Optimization Priorities:

1. **Add 2026 year markers** to all product comparison and "best of" pages — signals freshness for a competitive category with seasonal/new product launches.

2. **Use "Best" + number formula** for list pages (e.g., "Best Nicotine Pouches", "7 Strongest Brands 2026"). Dominates top 3 results.

3. **For geo pages (Germany, Austria)**, lead with **legality/regulatory clarity** — these keywords have high search intent for regulatory questions. Use "Legal 2026" or "What You Need to Know".

4. **For flavor pages** (e.g., "Mint", "ZYN Flavours"), use **"All" + "Ranked"** to signal completeness and editorial authority (not just catalog).

5. **Comparison titles** work best with **question frames** ("Which is Right for You?") — lowers friction for decision-making.

6. **For e-commerce keywords** ("Buy Online"), lead with **shipping promises** (Fast, Express, Free) — trust signal dominates.

7. **Avoid excessive length** — keep titles **50-60 characters** for mobile SERPs. Long subtitles after colons are Google's job, not yours.

8. **Reserve parenthetical notes** for legal/sensitive content only: "(For Adults Only)", "(2026 Edition)". Don't overuse.

- Prioritize 3-5 high-traffic pages (brand pages, "best of" guides, geo pages)
- A/B test new titles on GSC to measure CTR lift
- Update yearly (especially 2026 → 2027 transition)
