VELO vs LOOP: Which Is Better in 2026?

If you're shopping for premium nicotine pouches in 2026, you've likely noticed two names dominating European shelves: VELO and LOOP. Both are genuinely excellent products, but they represent two fundamentally different philosophies about what modern nicotine pouches should be. This guide cuts through the hype and helps you pick the right one for your habits.

Spoiler: It's not about which is objectively "better"—it's about which aligns with your flavor preferences, nicotine needs, and priorities around innovation vs. consistency.

The Companies Behind Them

VELO is made by British American Tobacco (BAT), the world's second-largest tobacco company. Yes, the corporation. That might make you skeptical, but here's why it matters: BAT has invested billions in heat-not-burn and reduced-risk products. VELO represents their commitment to quality control, global supply chains, and long-term R&D. Every can you buy has passed rigorous standards that corporate oversight demands.

LOOP comes from Another Snus Factory (ASF), an independent Swedish innovator. They're smaller, leaner, and obsessed with pushing boundaries—especially in flavor development and technology. If VELO is the reliable multinational, LOOP is the startup that takes risks and moves fast.

Neither approach is inherently superior. Corporate backing means consistency; indie DNA means experimentation. Choose based on what you value.

Product Range and Variety

This is where the philosophies diverge sharply.

VELO's lineup: 12 core products. They focus on perfecting a smaller set rather than overwhelming you with choice. You get Slim format exclusively, strengths from Zero (0mg) through MAX (17mg), and flavors that range from classic (Crispy Peppermint, Ice Cool) to modern (Ruby Berry, Tropical).

LOOP's lineup: 32+ products across three formats (Standard, Mini, Hyper). They're flavor-obsessed. Where VELO offers "Peppermint Crisp," LOOP gives you Habanero Mint, Jalapeño Lime, Red Chili Melon, and Mango. If you value choice and are willing to experiment, LOOP is overwhelming in the best way.

Winner for variety: LOOP by a landslide. But if choice paralysis is real for you, VELO's curated selection is a strength.

Strength and Nicotine Options

Both brands cater to users across the spectrum, but with different ceilings.

VELO strengths:

  • Zero (0mg) – for flavor-only users
  • Medium (6-8mg) – beginner-friendly
  • Strong (11-13mg) – daily users
  • MAX (17mg) – heavy users

LOOP strengths:

  • Zero (0mg) – for flavor-only users
  • Strong (12-15.6mg) – primary focus
  • No true "light" options – LOOP targets experienced users

The critical difference: VELO Freeze MAX at 17mg is genuinely the strongest pouch on the European market. If you need maximum kick, VELO wins. LOOP's highest is 15.6mg, and most products cluster at 12mg.

However, LOOP's Instant Rush technology (more below) means that 12-15.6mg LOOP often feels stronger than the equivalent VELO strength due to faster nicotine delivery.

Winner for maximum strength: VELO (17mg). Winner for perceived strength/delivery speed: LOOP (Instant Rush).

Flavor Profiles and Philosophy

This is where taste is entirely subjective, but the strategic differences are clear.

VELO flavors: Clean, sophisticated, slightly minty-heavy. Crispy Peppermint is their flagship—it's crisp, cooling, no weird aftertaste. Ice Cool leans into icy freshness. Freezing Peppermint MAX doubles down on that feeling. Ruby Berry, Tropical, and Grapefruit are their experiments, but they're restrained; they're designed to appeal to a broad audience.

LOOP flavors: Unapologetically bold and spicy. Habanero Mint sounds wild—and it is, but it works. Jalapeño Lime is actually refreshing, not just a gimmick. Red Chili Melon hits spice and sweetness at once. Mango is straightforward tropical. Mint Mania is an aggressive mint for purists.

If you want safe, classic flavors, VELO wins. If you want to try something genuinely different and aren't afraid of spice, LOOP is your brand.

Winner for flavor innovation: LOOP. Winner for crowd-pleasing reliability: VELO.

Instant Rush vs. Traditional Release

LOOP's Instant Rush technology is a real differentiator. It's a capsule system that releases nicotine faster—within seconds rather than minutes. This means:

  • Quicker satisfaction (important if you're using pouches to replace cigarettes)
  • Shorter duration (you'll finish the pouch effect faster)
  • More intense initial hit

VELO's traditional release is slower but longer-lasting. You get sustained nicotine delivery over 30+ minutes, which many users prefer for work or situations where you want consistent background stimulation.

This isn't a flaw—it's a choice. Instant Rush appeals to former smokers; traditional release appeals to users who want steady all-day support.

Winner: Depends on your use case. Instant Rush for quitting smoking; traditional for lifestyle nicotine.

Price and Value

The numbers are surprisingly close:

  • VELO: €4.08–€4.87 per can
  • LOOP: €4.17–€4.48 per can

Price is not a differentiator. Both are premium brands at similar price points. You're not paying extra for VELO's corporate backing, and you're not saving money on LOOP's indie status. The €0.30–€0.40 variance is retail noise.

Winner: Tie. Neither is cheaper. Buy based on preference.

Sustainability and Packaging

VELO: Traditional can design. Recyclable aluminum. Standard plastic lid. Function-first approach.

LOOP: Explicit focus on plant-based packaging and sustainability messaging. Smaller cans in some formats. More eco-conscious branding.

If you care deeply about environmental impact, LOOP communicates it more loudly. That said, both are recycled aluminum, so the practical difference is modest. LOOP's plant-based materials claims deserve a closer look at actual composition, but the intent is there.

Winner for messaging: LOOP. Winner for objective impact: negligible difference.

The Verdict

VELO is the better pouch if: You want maximum strength, corporate reliability, and classic flavors. It's the safe choice for beginners and users who value consistency above all.

LOOP is the better pouch if: You want flavor excitement, faster nicotine delivery, and product variety. It's the choice for experienced users willing to take a flavor risk.

The honest answer: Both are excellent. The "best" pouch depends entirely on whether you prioritize consistency (VELO) or innovation (LOOP). Many users buy both and rotate based on mood. VELO for a reliable Monday morning; LOOP for when you want spice on a Friday afternoon.

If you've only tried one, try the other. The differences are real, and they might surprise you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is VELO stronger than LOOP?

By raw nicotine content, yes—VELO MAX's 17mg beats LOOP's 15.6mg ceiling. But LOOP's Instant Rush technology makes 12-15.6mg LOOP feel stronger due to faster delivery. It depends whether you measure strength by dose or by perceived intensity.

Can I get nicotine without flavor?

Both VELO and LOOP offer zero-nic options. These are genuine zero-nicotine pouches for users who want oral stimulation without the drug.

Which has better quality control?

VELO, by virtue of BAT's corporate oversight. That said, LOOP maintains high standards—they're just a smaller company. We'd rate both as "premium quality"; VELO is slightly more rigid about consistency.

Are VELO and LOOP the same product with different branding?

No. They're completely different formulations, release technologies, flavor profiles, and companies. The confusion might arise because both are excellent, but they're genuinely distinct products.

Which is better for quitting smoking?

LOOP's Instant Rush better mimics the quick nicotine hit of a cigarette, making it slightly better for tobacco switchers. VELO's slower release is better for long-term nicotine maintenance once you've quit smoking.

Can I use both brands?

Absolutely. Many users rotate between VELO for routine and LOOP for variety. They're complementary, not competing.
