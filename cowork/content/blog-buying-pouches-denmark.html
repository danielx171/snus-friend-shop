Introduction

Denmark's nicotine market has undergone seismic shifts since the EU's snus ban took effect. Legal & Regulatory Landscape in Denmark

Understanding the law is your first step. Denmark's regulatory environment for nicotine pouches is now one of Europe's strictest—a stark contrast to the Wild West years of 2022-2024 when brands from around the world arrived unchecked.

The Snus Ban & Why It Doesn't Apply to Nicotine Pouches

Snus—moist tobacco product intended for oral use—has been banned in Denmark since 1992, decades before the EU's 2019 Tobacco Products Directive formalized the continent-wide prohibition. Sweden secured a historic exemption, but Denmark has no such carve-out. Snus remains fully illegal.

Nicotine pouches, however, contain no tobacco. This distinction allowed them to occupy a regulatory gray zone for years. Until 2024-2025, most European countries treated nicotine pouches as unregulated novel products. Denmark changed that approach in 2025.

July 2025: The Regulatory Shift

In July 2025, Denmark's Medicines Agency (Lægemiddelstyrelsen) and Ministry of Health (Sundhedsministeriet) introduced sweeping regulations:

  • Maximum 9mg nicotine per pouch. Products exceeding this threshold are banned from Danish sale. This eliminated the ultra-strong products (22-43mg) that appeal to heavy users.
  • Flavor ban: Only tobacco and menthol/mint flavors are permitted. Fruit, candy, spice, and dessert flavors—once the fastest-growing segment—are prohibited. This reflects public health policy aimed at reducing youth appeal.
  • Standardized packaging: Plain packaging requirements similar to cigarettes now apply. Bright designs are gone. Health warnings are mandatory.
  • Age verification mandate: Retailers must verify 18+ status at sale. Online retailers selling to Denmark must enforce this.
  • Registration requirement: Retailers must register with Danish health authorities to sell lawfully.

These rules apply to all nicotine pouches sold in Denmark, whether purchased domestically or imported. Products that fail to comply can be seized by Danish customs, and sellers who violate regulations face fines.

Skruf (Imperial Brands) maintains a loyal Scandinavian following. Their tobacco-flavored and mint options appeal to users prioritizing authenticity over novelty.

The Flavor Reality

Menthol and mint dominate now. Before July 2025, Danish users loved fruit flavors (strawberry, blueberry, watermelon) and dessert variants (vanilla, coffee, cinnamon). That market is gone domestically. Tobacco and tobacco-mint blends appeal to traditionalists. Expect limited range compared to international markets.

Strength Preferences

The 9mg ceiling shifted Danish preferences downward. Users who previously favored 22mg products had to choose: stick with domestic 9mg, or accept the customs risk of ordering international. Most adapted to 6-9mg. Newcomers are discovering that 3-6mg is adequate.
