## How It Works

1. **Submit your idea.** Describe what you want and why it matters -- one sentence is enough, but detail helps.
2. **The community votes.** Other members upvote the suggestions they want most. Popular ideas rise to the top.
3. **We build it.** Our team reviews the most-requested ideas each month and ships the best ones.
4. **You get rewarded.** If your suggestion ships, you earn the exclusive Pioneer badge and 200 SnusPoints.

---

## Guidelines

**What makes a great suggestion:**

- Be specific. "Add a flavour comparison tool" is better than "make the site better."
- Focus on something that would help you or other pouch users. Features, content, tools, UX improvements -- all fair game.
- Check existing suggestions first. If someone already posted your idea, upvote theirs instead of duplicating it.
- One idea per submission. If you have three ideas, submit three times.
- Keep it constructive. Criticism is welcome when it comes with a solution.

**What is out of scope:**

- Requests for specific product stock or pricing changes (that is a purchasing decision, not a feature)
- Anything that requires us to break our own policies (age verification, legal compliance, etc.)
- Personal support tickets -- use the [contact page](/contact) for order issues

---

## Rewards

The **Pioneer badge** is exclusive to users whose suggestions become real features. You cannot buy it, grind for it, or earn it any other way. When we ship a feature that came from your suggestion, you get:

- The Pioneer badge on your profile (permanent, visible on leaderboards)
- 200 SnusPoints credited to your account
- A shoutout in our monthly changelog

Your idea. Your badge. Simple as that.
