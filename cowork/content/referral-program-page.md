## FAQ Section

### Q: Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer?

No limit. Refer one friend or a hundred — you earn 100 SnusPoints for every friend who places their first order. There's no cap on referral earnings.

### Q: When do I receive my referral points?

Your 100 points are credited automatically as soon as your friend's first order is confirmed (typically within minutes of payment). You'll see them in your SnusPoints balance on your account page.

### Q: What does my friend get?

Your friend receives 50 SnusPoints the moment they create their account using your referral link or code. They can use those points immediately as a discount on their first order.

### Q: Can I refer someone who already has an account?

No — the referral bonus applies to new accounts only. If your friend already has a SnusFriend account, they won't receive the signup bonus and you won't earn referral points. But you can still share products you love with them.

### Q: Do my referral points expire?

SnusPoints never expire as long as your account is active. If your account is inactive for 12 consecutive months (no logins or orders), points may be forfeited — but we'll warn you by email well in advance.

### Q: Can I use referral points together with other discounts?

Yes. SnusPoints discounts stack with subscription savings and promotional offers. The only exception is that points can't be applied to shipping costs.
