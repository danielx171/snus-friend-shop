Legal & Regulatory Landscape: What You Need to Know

Austria's regulatory framework for nicotine pouches is complex and evolving. Nicotine pouches are classified as "tobacco-related products" under the Tabak- und Nichtraucherinnen- bzw. Nichtraucherschutzgesetz (TNRSG—Tobacco and Non-Smoker Protection Act), which means they fall under tobacco regulations rather than being treated as a separate product category.

Age Restrictions

As of 2025, nicotine pouches can only be purchased by individuals 18 years or older throughout Austria. This represents a tightening from earlier regulations in some Bundesländer (federal states) where 16-year-olds were permitted to buy. Age verification is mandatory at all licensed Trafiken and authorized online retailers.

Sales Channels

Nicotine pouches in Austria can only be sold through two channels:

  1. Licensed Trafiken: Austria's ~4,300 official tobacco shops, overseen by the Monopolverwaltung GmbH (MVG—Austrian Tobacco Monopoly Administration)
  2. Authorized online retailers: Websites that have registered with the MVG and comply with age verification and packaging regulations

Importation for personal use from EU warehouses (such as those in Germany, the Netherlands, or Sweden) exists in a grey legal area but is generally tolerated for small quantities (typically under 5 cans per order).

Excise Tax & Pricing

An excise tax on nicotine pouches was introduced in Austria in 2025, increasing the base price at retail. Trafiken now typically sell cans at €4.50–€6.00 each, reflecting both the tax and standard retail margins. Online retailers based in other EU countries (with lower tax rates) can offer significantly cheaper alternatives, which is why cross-border ordering has become increasingly popular among Austrian consumers.
