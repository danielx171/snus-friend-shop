## RAVE Nicotine Pouches: The Complete Lineup

### Strong (16mg/can — 9.6 mg/pouch)

RAVE's "Strong" tier is their entry point for nicotine users. At 9.6 mg per pouch, it sits in the solid mid-range — comparable to ZYN S3 (9.0 mg) or slightly stronger.

| Product | Flavour Profile | Taste Notes |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **Frozen Citrus 16mg** | Sharp citrus with icy cooling | Clean, crisp opening that builds. The "frozen" element is genuine — there's a menthol-like cooling that lingers. One of the most refreshing citrus pouches on the market. |
| **Lemoncello 16mg** | Italian-inspired lemon zest | Distinctly different from generic "lemon" — it's got a sweet, almost dessert-like quality that references actual limoncello. Unique in the category. |
| **Mango Django 16mg** | Tropical mango | Sweet, ripe mango with no artificial edge. The "Django" branding is fun. Mango flavour holds up well over the 30-minute session — doesn't fade to nothing after 10 minutes. |
| **La Passion Fruit 16mg** | Exotic passionfruit | Tart and tropical with good complexity. Not as common a flavour in the pouch world, which makes it a good change of pace from mint. |
| **Juicy Apple 16mg** | Crisp green apple | Orchard-fresh apple that avoids the "candy apple" trap. Clean and natural-tasting. |
| **Minty Berry 16mg** | Mixed berry with cool mint | A dual-flavour pouch that actually works. The berry is present, the mint provides backbone. Good daily driver. |
| **Merry Cherry 16mg** | Bright cherry | Sweet without being cloying. Lighter than some cherry pouches that veer into cough-syrup territory. |
| **Cola Explosion 16mg** | Bold cola | This one's a love-it-or-hate-it. If you like cola sweets (think Haribo Cola Bottles), you'll enjoy this. Otherwise, it's an odd experience in a pouch. |
| **Skittler 16mg** | Rainbow fruit mix | The wildcard. It's literally a "taste the rainbow" pouch — multiple fruit notes blended together. Fun, sweet, and genuinely different from anything else in the category. |

**Overall Strong tier rating:** 7.5/10. Good flavour execution, consistent nicotine delivery, and genuinely creative flavour choices. The 9.6 mg strength is appropriate for the majority of regular users.

### Ultra Strong (32.5mg/can — 19.5 mg/pouch)

RAVE's Ultra Strong line doubles down on their creative flavours at a significantly higher nicotine level. At 19.5 mg per pouch, these sit between ZYN's maximum (16.5 mg) and the mid-range extreme brands (25+ mg).

| Product | Flavour Profile | Taste Notes |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **Frozen Citrus 32.5mg** | Same citrus-ice profile, stronger hit | The flavour is identical to the 16mg version but the nicotine buzz is noticeable within 2–3 minutes. Good for experienced users who loved the Strong version. |
| **Lemoncello 32.5mg** | Same limoncello sweetness, bigger payload | Still unique, still good. The higher nicotine doesn't overpower the flavour. |
| **Mango Django 32.5mg** | Tropical mango, ultra strength | The mango holds up at this strength — impressive flavour engineering. |
| **La Passion Fruit 32.5mg** | Intense passionfruit | The tartness pairs well with the stronger nicotine hit. |
| **Juicy Apple 32.5mg** | Crisp apple, amplified | Clean apple at higher strength. |
| **Minty Berry 32.5mg** | Berry-mint at ultra strength | The mint intensifies at this level, which actually improves the balance. |
| **Merry Cherry 32.5mg** | Cherry at full power | Holds up well. Cherry flavour doesn't fade. |
| **Cola Explosion 32.5mg** | Ultra cola | The cola flavour with a serious nicotine kick. For cola pouch enthusiasts. |
| **Skittler 32.5mg** | Maximum rainbow | Every bit as fun as the 16mg version but with a genuine strength profile. |

**Overall Ultra Strong tier rating:** 7/10. Same great flavours at a strength that puts them in genuine "strong" territory. The 19.5 mg level is a sweet spot — strong enough to satisfy experienced users, not so extreme that flavour is lost.

## RAVE Energy Pouches: Caffeine Without Nicotine

This is where RAVE does something genuinely different. Their energy line contains zero nicotine — instead, each can contains 80–100 mg of caffeine (roughly equivalent to one cup of coffee, spread across 20 pouches).

| Product | Caffeine/Can | Flavour |
|---------|-------------|---------|
| **Mint Man 100mg** | 100 mg | Cool mint |
| **Bubble Gun 100mg** | 100 mg | Sweet bubblegum |
| **Watermelon Ice 100mg** | 100 mg | Juicy watermelon + ice |
| **Strawberry Shake 100mg** | 100 mg | Creamy strawberry |
| **Grapper 100mg** | 100 mg | Grape-inspired |
| **Frozen Citrus 80mg** | 80 mg | Icy citrus |
| **Minty Berry 80mg** | 80 mg | Berry + mint |
| **La Passion Fruit 80mg** | 80 mg | Tropical passionfruit |

**Who are these for?**
- People who want the oral pouch experience without nicotine
- Students or professionals wanting discreet caffeine delivery
- Nicotine users looking for a daytime alternative to reduce intake
- People tapering off nicotine who want to maintain the pouch habit

**Rating:** 6.5/10. The concept is smart and execution is decent. Caffeine absorption through oral mucosa is real but slower than coffee. These won't replace your morning espresso, but they're a genuinely interesting alternative for specific use cases.

## Verdict: Who Should Buy RAVE?

**Buy RAVE if you:**
- Want unique flavours you can't find elsewhere (Lemoncello, Skittler, Mango Django)
- Need a brand with both nicotine and caffeine options
- Are an experienced user looking for 9.6–19.5 mg strength
- Enjoy fruit-forward flavours over mint
- Want to reduce nicotine intake by alternating with energy pouches

**Look elsewhere if you:**
- Need extreme strength (30+ mg) — try CUBA, Siberia, or Rabbit
- Want the gentlest possible introduction — try ZYN Mini S1 or S2
- Prefer traditional mint-dominant flavours — try VELO or ZYN
- Need the widest product range — ZYN (52 products) or VELO (53 products) offer more variety
