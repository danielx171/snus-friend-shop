## Introduction

ZYN uses a numbered strength system (S1 through S6) that's straightforward once you understand it — but confusing if you're comparing across formats. A ZYN Mini S2 contains different total nicotine than a ZYN Slim S2, even though both carry the same "S" label.

## ZYN Strength System Explained

ZYN's "S" system indicates relative strength within a format — not an absolute nicotine value:

| S-Rating | Strength Label | Typical mg/pouch (Slim) | Typical mg/pouch (Mini) |
|----------|---------------|------------------------|------------------------|
| S1 | Light | — | 1.5 mg |
| S2 | Regular | 6.0 mg | 3.0 mg |
| S3 | Strong | 9.0 mg | — |
| S4 | Extra Strong | 10.6–11.0 mg | 6.0 mg |
| S5 | Super Strong | 13.5 mg | — |
| S6 | Ultra Strong | 16.5 mg | — |

**Key insight:** A Mini S4 (6.0 mg) is weaker than a Slim S2 (6.0 mg) — they're the same nicotine, but the S-rating only compares within the same format. Always check mg/pouch, not just the S number.

## How to Choose Your ZYN Strength

### Step 1: Assess Your Nicotine Background

| Your Background | Recommended Start | Notes |
|----------------|-------------------|-------|
| Never used nicotine | S1 Mini (1.5 mg) | Use 2–3/day for first week |
| Social smoker (<5/day) | S2 Mini (3 mg) | 4–5/day |
| Regular smoker (5–15/day) | S2 Slim (6 mg) or S3 (9 mg) | 5–8/day |
| Heavy smoker (15+/day) | S3 (9 mg) or S4 (11 mg) | 6–10/day |
| Existing pouch user (6 mg+) | Match your current level | Check mg/pouch, not S-rating |

### Step 2: Choose Your Format

- **Mini:** Smaller, more discreet. Lower mg per pouch. Better for light users or those wanting subtlety.
- **Slim:** Standard size. Higher mg per pouch. Better for most regular users.

### Step 3: Dial In Over 2 Weeks

Don't judge your ZYN strength after one pouch. Give yourself 7–14 days at a consistent level before deciding to increase. Your body needs time to adjust to oral nicotine absorption patterns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What mg is ZYN 6?
ZYN's "6" can refer to their S6 strength tier (16.5 mg/pouch in Slim) or the 6 mg/pouch products (Slim S2). In the US market, ZYN uses "6 mg" to label their stronger option (6 mg/pouch). In the European market, the S-rating system is used. Always check the package for mg/pouch.

### Which ZYN is strongest?
ZYN Cool Mint Ice Slim S6 and ZYN Menthol Ice Slim S6, both at 16.5 mg/pouch. These are ZYN's "Ultra Strong" tier.

### Is ZYN S3 the same as ZYN 3 mg?
No. ZYN S3 (Strong) contains 9 mg/pouch in Slim format. ZYN "3 mg" refers to their Mini S2 products with 3 mg/pouch. The naming is confusing — always check the actual mg/pouch figure.

### Can I use ZYN to quit smoking?
Many people use ZYN as a smoking replacement. Start at a strength that matches your cigarette consumption (see the table above), maintain for 2–4 weeks, then consider gradually stepping down. ZYN's range from 1.5 mg to 16.5 mg allows for a controlled tapering journey.
